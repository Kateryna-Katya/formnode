Hollow Peak Lodge

About the Game

Survival driven by tension and isolation.
The game combines exploration, environmental storytelling, and psychological tension rather than direct combat. Players search for clues hidden throughout the lodge while dealing with freezing temperatures, unstable electricity, and increasingly strange events. We want you creeped out, not constantly jumping out of your seat.

Features

  • Environmental Storytelling

    The narrative unfolds through abandoned rooms, personal belongings, radio messages, and hidden documents.

  • Resource Management

    Players manage warmth, light sources, and limited supplies.

  • Psychological Events

    The lodge subtly changes over time, creating uncertainty about what is real.

  • Cinematic Horror Atmosphere

    Heavy snowfall, wooden interiors, candlelight, and distant ambient sounds create constant tension.
